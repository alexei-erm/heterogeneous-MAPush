- Implemented in `go1_object.py` `_create_npc()`

#### 2f. Contact-Force Gating Effectiveness on Velocity Task
- **alpha=0.1**: Agents push from opposite sides, glued to box. Over-gating causes "don't lose contact" optimization. Box barely moves.
- **alpha=0.5**: No coordination at all. Meaningless pushing.
- **Conclusion**: Contact-force gating may not suit velocity task. Cosine similarity + angular velocity penalty already provide cooperation signals. The gating creates pathological incentives in this task structure.

## Discussion: Heterogeneous Collaboration Problem

### Observation
User tested Go1+Go1 on velocity task (legacy checkpoint, 2 Go1s) — works great, both agents collaborate. But when training Go1+ANYmal_C on go1push_mid, Go1 becomes a freeloader because ANYmal is strong enough to push solo.

### Proposed Solutions (ordered by recommendation)

1. **Heavy box mass randomization (try first)**
   - `--box_mass_range 8 30` — upper end too heavy for ANYmal alone
   - Forces HAPPO to learn cooperation as the only way to solve heavy-box episodes
   - Most natural solution, no reward engineering

2. **Approach velocity bonus (reward-based)**
   - Small positive reward when both agents near box and box moving toward goal
   - Less aggressive than gating — makes collaboration attractive rather than punishing freeloading
   - Already planned in session_2026-03-08.md but not yet implemented

3. **Contact-force gating with high alpha (0.7-0.8)**
   - Gentle nudge (max 30% reward reduction for freeloader)
   - Mass normalization fix from 2026-03-07 accounts for ANYmal's strength advantage
   - May work better on go1push_mid than velocity task

4. **Mass randomization + gentle gating combined**
   - Belt and suspenders approach
